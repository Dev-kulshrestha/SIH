The Reality & The Innovation

Every ride should feel safe.

Current ride-sharing platforms focus almost entirely on GPS tracking, leaving driver fatigue and in-cabin safety unaddressed.

The Problem

Traditional rides leave critical blind spots.

Long working hours and late-night trips introduce unseen vulnerabilities for passengers and drivers alike.

  • Driver fatigue increases accident risk: Sleep deprivation slows reaction time just like intoxication.
  • Drivers don't realize drowsiness: Micro-sleeps happen in split seconds without conscious awareness.
  • Hardware barriers limit adoption: Expensive dedicated automotive sensors are cost-prohibitive for fleets.
  • Detection without response fails: Simple warnings mean nothing without connected emergency support.
The WideEYE Solution

Intelligent vision meets instant intervention.

WideEYE transforms any smartphone or vehicle camera into an intelligent active safety copilot.

  • Camera-based driver monitoring: Uses existing standard optical devices without costly hardware.
  • Real-time drowsiness detection: Computes eye-closure and yawn metrics to detect fatigue instantly.
  • Intelligent safety alerts: Escalating audio and haptic cues help drivers regain alertness promptly.
  • Automated emergency assistance: Triggers emergency SOS triage when critical risk is detected.
