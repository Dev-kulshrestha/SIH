What is WideEYE?

An AI-powered ride experience designed with safety at its core.

WideEYE is a ride-booking and driver safety platform that combines everyday mobility with real-time AI-based driver fatigue monitoring. It helps identify signs of drowsiness while a ride is in progress and provides timely alerts before the situation becomes critical.

The Reality & The Innovation

Why WideEYE?

Current ride platforms focus primarily on navigation, leaving driver fatigue and in-cabin safety unaddressed.

The Challenge

Driver fatigue is difficult to recognize before it becomes dangerous.

During long or night journeys, drivers can experience reduced alertness, prolonged eye closure, frequent yawning and changes in head position. A driver may not always recognize these signs early enough.

  • Micro-sleeps happen in split seconds without conscious awareness.
  • Sleep deprivation impairs reflexes just like alcohol intoxication.
  • Proprietary sensors are cost-prohibitive for everyday transit fleets.
The WideEYE Solution

WideEYE adds an intelligent safety layer to the ride.

Instead of waiting for an incident, WideEYE continuously analyzes available facial signals to identify increasing signs of fatigue and provide an early warning.

  • Edge-computed vision AI running locally on camera devices.
  • Progressive multi-stage warnings before critical risk occurs.
  • Automated emergency SOS and live triage response support.

Computer Vision & Facial Analysis

Inside the AI

Multiple signals. One safety decision.

WideEYE does not depend on simple eye detection alone. It tracks multiple synchronized facial cues in real time to build a robust assessment.

EYE ACTIVITY

Tracks eye openness and changes in eye activity using facial landmarks.

BLINK ANALYSIS

Analyzes blink duration and patterns over time instead of reacting to a single blink.

MOUTH MOVEMENT

Uses Mouth Aspect Ratio (MAR) and mouth movement as an additional fatigue signal.

HEAD POSITION

Analyzes changes in head orientation and position that may indicate reduced alertness.

WideEYE combines these signals over time to estimate a drowsiness risk score.

Progressive Escalation

When Risk Increases

WideEYE executes a clear multi-tiered intervention protocol to protect both driver and passenger.

STATE 01 · SAFE

Visual Monitoring

Silent background camera monitoring with live telemetry tracking baseline alertness.

STATE 02 · WARNING

Driver Alert

Subtle visual HUD notifications and gentle sound cues prompt the driver to refocus.

STATE 03 · HIGH RISK

Stronger Safety Alert

Escalating dual-frequency chime, passenger app alert, and rest break suggestion.

STATE 04 · CRITICAL

SOS RESPONSE

5-second automated countdown triggers emergency contact alerts and location broadcast.
